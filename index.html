About Us

Redefining Cardiovascular
Medicine with AI

We are an interdisciplinary research laboratory at the forefront of applying artificial intelligence to cardiovascular medicine, bridging the gap between computational innovation and clinical practice.

Vision

To become a world-leading center for AI-powered cardiovascular research, enabling precision medicine through intelligent analysis and prediction systems.

Mission

Develop cutting-edge AI algorithms and tools that empower clinicians, accelerate cardiac research, and ultimately improve patient outcomes globally.

Values

Scientific rigor, clinical relevance, open collaboration, ethical AI, and a relentless commitment to translating research into real-world impact.

01

Multi-Modal Data Fusion

Integrating ECG, echocardiography, cardiac MRI, CT angiography, and electronic health records for comprehensive cardiovascular analysis.

02

Clinical Translation

From benchside to bedside — our models are validated in real clinical environments with direct feedback from cardiologists.

03

Open Science

Committed to reproducible research with open-source code, shared datasets, and transparent methodology documentation.

Research Areas

Core Research Directions

Our research spans the full spectrum of AI-driven cardiovascular medicine, from fundamental algorithm development to clinical decision support systems.

ECG Intelligent Analysis

Deep learning-based ECG interpretation for arrhythmia detection, myocardial infarction prediction, and automated cardiac rhythm classification with clinical-grade accuracy.

Deep Learning Signal Processing Arrhythmia

Cardiac Imaging AI

Automated segmentation, quantification, and disease detection in echocardiography, cardiac MRI, and CT angiography using state-of-the-art computer vision models.

Computer Vision Segmentation MRI/CT

Risk Prediction Models

Machine learning models for cardiovascular risk stratification, heart failure prognosis, and adverse event prediction using multi-dimensional clinical data.

Predictive Analytics Risk Scoring Prognosis

Genomics & Biomarkers
